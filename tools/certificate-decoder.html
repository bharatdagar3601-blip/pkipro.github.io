Standard PEM-encoded X.509 certificates (the -----BEGIN CERTIFICATE----- format).

Can I decode a certificate chain?

Paste the leaf certificate here for full details; use the Bulk Decoder or Chain Sorter tool for multi-certificate chains.

Yes — nothing is uploaded, decoding happens entirely in your browser.
